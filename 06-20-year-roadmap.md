# 06 — 20-Year Roadmap

The end state: **developing brand-new housing at scale**, funded and de-risked by everything built along the way. Each phase creates the capital, network, and skills the next phase needs.

## Phase 1 — Operator & Concierge (Years 0–2)
- Launch Inner Circle on 2 days/week; realtor work continues full-time.
- Own portfolio becomes the flagship demo; every client system tested at home first.
- Realtor funnel active from day one ([04](04-realtor-funnel.md)).
- **Exit criteria:** $4–5K/mo recurring, 12+ retainer clients, 2+ funnel-sourced transactions closed.

## Phase 2 — Coach & Productizer (Years 2–5)
- Add Day C; recurring revenue passes realtor take-home on a per-hour basis.
- Productize: recorded course, template/prompt vault, group program — income that doesn't consume calendar days.
- Decide deliberately whether realtor stays full-time or becomes funnel-driven only.
- **Exit criteria:** $10K+/mo combined concierge income, a course/community that sells without 1:1 delivery, 3+ years of operating data.

## Phase 3 — Portfolio Builder (Years 5–10)
- Redirect concierge cash flow + realtor commissions into acquisitions: small multifamily, BRRRR, mid-term rental conversions.
- The Inner Circle community matures into an **investor network** — partners, private lenders, and co-investors who already trust the brand.
- Systems built in Phases 1–2 mean each new door adds almost no operating hours.
- **Exit criteria:** 15–25 doors (or equivalent equity), first partnership/syndication deal completed, relationships with lenders and contractors.

## Phase 4 — Developer (Years 10–20)
- Move up the value chain: land acquisition, entitlements, ground-up small multifamily, then larger projects.
- Everything compounds here: realtor license (deal access & disposition), operator experience (build what actually rents), capital (portfolio equity), and community (the investor pipeline from the Inner Circle brand).
- Start small — a duplex or ADU project — before scaling to subdivisions or apartment buildings.
- **The 20-year outcome:** creating new housing stock at scale, with Adventure Family Getaways as operator/brand and Inner Circle as the capital-and-knowledge community around it.

## The through-line

Every phase sells the *result* of the previous phase: Phase 1 sells operating expertise, Phase 2 sells the system, Phase 3 deploys the proceeds, Phase 4 builds what Phase 3 proved the market wants. Nothing is wasted — the $497 Quick Start client in year one can be a co-investor in year twelve.
