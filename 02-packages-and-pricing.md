# 02 — Packages & Pricing

Every package is priced so the effective hourly rate is **above** the $55/hr base, while total hours are capped by a defined scope. $55/hr survives only as the **founding-client legacy rate** (first 3 clients, testimonial required — see [07 — Launch Checklist](07-launch-checklist.md)).

## One-time setup packages

| Package | Price | Scope | Est. hours | Effective rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Quick Start** | **$497** | 1 property | ~6 hrs | ~$83/hr |
| **Full Concierge Install** | **$1,497** | Up to 3 properties | ~15 hrs | ~$100/hr |
| **Portfolio System** | **$2,997** | Whole portfolio | ~25 hrs | ~$120/hr |

### Quick Start — $497
- AI guest-messaging assistant for 1 property (Claude/ChatGPT-powered, integrated with the client's PMS/channel or template-based)
- Digital guidebook (check-in, house manual, local recommendations)
- FAQ knowledge base built from the property's real guest questions
- 1-hour handoff call

### Full Concierge Install — $1,497
- Everything in Quick Start, for up to 3 properties
- Automated message sequences: booking confirmation → pre-arrival → check-in → mid-stay → checkout → review request
- Upsell messages (early check-in, late checkout, extend-your-stay)
- **Computer/workstation setup**: machine configured, password manager, cloud backup, all AI tools installed and signed in
- 1 live training session (90 min)

### Portfolio System — $2,997
- Everything in Full Concierge Install, portfolio-wide (up to 8 properties; larger by custom quote)
- Owner dashboard (bookings, messages, task tracking in one place)
- Written SOPs for cleaners/co-hosts + team training session
- 30 days of post-install support included
- Ends with a **Portfolio Growth Audit** — a natural bridge to the realtor conversation ([04](04-realtor-funnel.md))

## Monthly retainers — the consistent income

| Tier | Price | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Inner Circle Membership** | **$97/mo** | Monthly group coaching call, prompt & template library, private community, priority booking for services |
| **Concierge Care** | **$297/mo** | System monitoring & tweaks, monthly optimization pass, up to 2 support hours |
| **Inner Circle VIP** | **$597/mo** | Everything above + monthly 1:1 strategy session, hands-on updates, quarterly system audit, **first access to deal flow** |

Retainer rules:
- Care and VIP require a completed setup package (they maintain what was installed).
- Membership is open to anyone — it's the low-friction entry point and the lead pool for setups.
- Unused support hours don't roll over; scope creep gets quoted as a new project.

## Coaching (à la carte)

- **Single session: $75/hr** (the new walk-up rate — $55 is retired to legacy status after the founding clients)
- **4-session pack: $260** ($65/session — prepaid, use within 90 days)
- Topics: AI tools for hosts, listing optimization, pricing strategy, systems & automation, getting started in STR

## Revenue scenarios

Assumes 2 working days/week (~16 hrs), growing to 3 days at the Target stage.

| | Conservative (mo. 6) | Target (mo. 18) | Stretch (mo. 30) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setups/month | 1 (avg $1,000) | 2 (avg $1,500) | 3 (avg $1,700) |
| Membership @$97 | 5 → $485 | 20 → $1,940 | 40 → $3,880 |
| Care @$297 | 2 → $594 | 10 → $2,970 | 15 → $4,455 |
| VIP @$597 | 0 | 3 → $1,791 | 6 → $3,582 |
| **One-time** | **$1,000** | **$3,000** | **$5,100** |
| **Recurring** | **$1,079** | **$6,701** | **$11,917** |
| **Total/month** | **~$2,100** | **~$9,700** | **~$17,000** |

At Target, recurring revenue alone (~$6.7K/mo ≈ $80K/yr) is a real income floor before any realtor commissions — that's the point where replacing the full-time realtor schedule becomes a choice, not a necessity. Capacity limits that keep these numbers honest are in [05 — Weekly Schedule](05-weekly-schedule.md).

## Pricing principles

1. **Never sell unbundled hours to new clients.** Hours are what the packages contain, not what's on the menu.
2. **Raise prices every ~10 clients** or when booked 3+ weeks out.
3. **Anchor high.** Present Portfolio System first; most buyers land on Full Install.
4. **Retainer-first mindset.** Every setup conversation ends with the Care/VIP offer while the value is fresh.
