# 04 — Realtor Funnel

The concierge business is not a distraction from real estate — it is a lead machine for it. Every Inner Circle client is, by definition, a rental property investor. Investors buy and sell property.

## Why this funnel is unusually good

- Concierge clients already trust the operator with their business systems — the hardest part of a realtor relationship (trust) is pre-built.
- Working inside their portfolio reveals exactly when they're ready to buy (cash flow improving, wanting to scale) or sell (burnout, underperformers).
- Coaching students who *want* to get into STR need to buy their first property — the cleanest buyer lead there is.

## Funnel mechanisms

1. **VIP deal flow perk.** Inner Circle VIP ($597/mo) includes first access to on- and off-market rental-suitable listings. The perk itself is the realtor pipeline.
2. **Portfolio Growth Audit.** Every Portfolio System install ends with one: which properties over/under-perform, what the equity position looks like, and what acquiring door #N would take. It is genuinely useful — and it surfaces buy/sell intent.
3. **Referral credit.** A closed real-estate referral earns the client a free month of Concierge Care. Cheap for the LLC, meaningful to the client.
4. **Email nurture.** Monthly note to the whole list: one AI/hosting tip + one local market insight. Stays useful, keeps the realtor identity present without pitching.
5. **Coaching → first purchase.** "Getting started in STR" coaching naturally terminates in a property search.

## Compliance guardrails

- Keep the LLC's concierge/coaching services and brokerage activity **clearly separated**: separate invoices, separate branding on transactional documents.
- Disclose the realtor relationship in writing whenever a concierge client becomes a real-estate client, per state rules and brokerage policy.
- No fee-splitting or referral payments that violate RESPA or state license law — the Care-month credit applies to *concierge* services, and should be confirmed against brokerage/state rules before launch.
- Check with the broker that outside business activity is documented.

## Measuring it

Track in a simple sheet: client → source → package(s) → realtor conversation opened? → transaction closed → commission. Even **one closed transaction per year** from the funnel (~$8–12K typical commission) roughly doubles the Conservative-scenario concierge income. Two to three per year at Target scale is realistic once VIP and coaching tiers fill.
