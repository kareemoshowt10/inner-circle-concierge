# 07 — Launch Checklist (First 90 Days)

## Days 1–14: Foundation
- [ ] Confirm LLC covers concierge/coaching services (or add a DBA "Inner Circle Concierge"); business bank account separated
- [ ] Confirm outside-business-activity policy with brokerage; note compliance guardrails from [04](04-realtor-funnel.md)
- [ ] Set up payments (Stripe/Square) with the package prices from [02](02-packages-and-pricing.md)
- [ ] Create the scheduling link limited to Day A / Day B slots ([05](05-weekly-schedule.md))
- [ ] Simple intake form + client tracking sheet (client, source, package, hours logged, funnel status)

## Days 15–30: Build the demo
- [ ] Run the full [playbook](03-ai-concierge-playbook.md) on the own portfolio — every property
- [ ] Capture before/after: guest response times, hours saved per week, screenshots
- [ ] Write the one-page offer sheet (three setup packages + retainers)
- [ ] Add an "Inner Circle" page or section to adventurefamilygetaways.com (landing page proper is a later phase)

## Days 31–60: Founding clients
- [ ] Recruit **3 founding clients at the $55/hr legacy rate** (capped hours per playbook checklist) — sourced from host groups, realtor sphere, and past guests who own rentals
- [ ] Deliver all 3 installs; collect a testimonial + measurable result from each
- [ ] Offer each founding client Concierge Care at $297/mo on handoff
- [ ] Post one case study per install (social + email list)

## Days 61–90: Switch to packages & open the Inner Circle
- [ ] Retire hourly for new clients; all new business quotes package pricing
- [ ] Launch **Inner Circle Membership at $97/mo** — first group coaching call on a Day B; founding clients get month one free
- [ ] Raise coaching walk-up rate to $75/hr
- [ ] Book the next 2 setup projects at full package price
- [ ] Review: hours actually spent vs. package scopes; adjust estimates before scaling

## 90-day success criteria

- 3 installs delivered with testimonials
- At least 1 Care subscriber and 5 Membership subscribers (≈ $780+/mo recurring)
- 2 package-priced projects booked
- 1 realtor-funnel conversation opened
- Everything delivered inside 2 days/week

Miss two or more of these → diagnose (offer, audience, or capacity) before spending on marketing. Hit them → follow the growth curve in [02](02-packages-and-pricing.md) toward the Target scenario.
