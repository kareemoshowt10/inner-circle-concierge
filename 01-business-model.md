# 01 — Business Model

## What Inner Circle is

A concierge + implementation + coaching brand under Adventure Family Getaways LLC. Three things are sold:

1. **AI concierge installs** — done-for-you setup of AI guest messaging, automations, and digital guidebooks for STR/LTR properties.
2. **Computer & tech setup** — the owner's workstation, tools, accounts, and security configured properly.
3. **Coaching & membership** — teaching owners to run the systems themselves, plus an ongoing "Inner Circle" community.

## Who buys

**Primary customer: other STR/LTR owners and hosts.** Self-managing hosts with 1–10 doors who are overwhelmed by guest messaging and tech, and small portfolio owners who want systems before they scale.

**Secondary beneficiary: guests of the Adventure Family Getaways portfolio.** The own portfolio runs the exact systems being sold — it is the live demo, the case study, and the testimonial engine. Every improvement made for clients gets tested at home first.

## The moat

Nobody else in the local market is all three of these at once:

- **Operator** — owns and runs an STR/LTR portfolio (adventurefamilygetaways.com)
- **Realtor** — licensed, transacting, knows the market and the deals
- **Implementer** — actually installs the AI tools, not just talks about them

A tech consultant doesn't understand hosting. A hosting coach can't set up the computer. Neither can sell the client their next property. Inner Circle can.

## Why packages, not hours

At $55/hr, income is capped by hours worked and every week starts at zero. Packages fix both problems:

- **Project pricing** decouples price from hours — clients pay for the outcome (a working AI concierge), and efficiency gains go to the business, not the client.
- **Monthly retainers** create recurring revenue — income that shows up before the week starts, which is the only path to "consistent income on 2–3 days/week."

Full menu and math in [02 — Packages & Pricing](02-packages-and-pricing.md).

## Revenue model at a glance

Three income streams, stacked:

| Stream | Type | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Setup packages ($497–$2,997) | One-time | Cash now + creates retainer clients |
| Retainers ($97–$597/mo) | Recurring | The consistent-income backbone |
| Realtor commissions | Episodic, large | Funded by the funnel in [04](04-realtor-funnel.md) |

Target trajectory (detailed scenarios in doc 02): reach **$4–5K/mo recurring within 18–24 months** on 2 days/week, at which point a third day gets added and the "replace the realtor day job" conversation becomes real — especially since the concierge business *generates* realtor deals rather than competing with them.
